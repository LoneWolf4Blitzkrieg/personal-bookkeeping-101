Personal Bookkeeping 101

Learn to follow every dollar of your paycheck on a real double-entry ledger page, by hand or on screen. Money is a system with rules, and once you know the rules there is nothing to fear.

Start Module 1

What you will be able to do

  • Keep your own books on double-entry ledger paper, and prove they balance.
  • Budget with logic instead of emotion, by deciding where each dollar goes before you spend it.
  • Plan for the future and build an emergency fund.
  • Understand how the Federal Reserve, inflation and IRAs affect your money (add-on modules).

How the course works

  • 16 modulesplus 3 add-on modules
  • 2.5 hoursper module: 1 hour of lessons, 90 minutes of practice
  • About 8 weeksat two modules a week
  • Your own pacereread any step as often as you need

Every example is a page of ledger paper. Each module ends with a short check, and you move on once you have shown you understand it. If you need more time, take it; if you already know the early material, a placement test can move you ahead.
